# fxverify — evidence pack demo

**See what a strategy robustness evidence pack looks like before you sell or trade an EA or Python strategy.**

This is the public showcase for [fxverify](https://q00tar.gumroad.com/l/wnplqk) — a local,
buy-once tool that turns a backtest of *any* OHLCV strategy (FX, stocks, ETFs,
crypto) into a reproducible robustness report you can publish next to your
product page, README, or client hand-off.

> **This does not certify profitability; it documents robustness checks.**
> An evidence pack tells a buyer *how honestly a strategy was tested* — it does
> not claim the strategy will make money.

## The red-flag checklist (read this first)

Every evidence pack leads with three checks that answer, at a glance, whether a
backtest is even worth believing:

| Red flag | Question it answers | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Min-trades gate** | Did any fold trade too little to mean anything? | A "great" result on 3 trades is noise, not an edge. |
| **Parameter OOS drift** | Does the edge survive neighboring parameter sets? | If the verdict flips one parameter step away, you curve-fit. |
| **Look-ahead risk** | Do signals stay stable when the future is truncated? | Catches accidental use of not-yet-known data. |

Full explanation with worked examples: [`RED_FLAGS.md`](RED_FLAGS.md).

### What the failing pack shows

The sample SMA-crossover strategy is **intentionally naive**, so it *fails* — and
that is the point. In [`evidence_pack/fail/report.md`](evidence_pack/fail/report.md):

- **Verdict: `FAIL_STRATEGY`** — printed at the top, no burying the lede.
- **Parameter OOS drift: FLAG** — `0/10` neighboring parameter sets passed; the
  verdict flips outside the tuned point. This is the classic overfit signature.
- Per-fold PF / max DD / Sharpe / trade counts, explicit fail reasons, and the
  spread/fee assumptions used — so a reader can see exactly why it failed.

A passing strategy ([`evidence_pack/pass/report.md`](evidence_pack/pass/report.md))
clears the same gates on a trending sample, for contrast.

## Reproduce it yourself

The pack in this repo was generated by fxverify from the committed sample data.
With fxverify installed you get the same output, including matching
input/config hashes:

```bash
# Regenerate the fail + pass evidence pack into ./out
fxverify pack ./out

# Or summarize your own exported trades / MT5 deals CSV
fxverify trades my_deals.csv

# Or an MT5 optimization results CSV/TSV
fxverify optimization opt_results.csv
```

The reproducibility hashes printed at the bottom of every report let anyone
confirm the report was produced from the stated data and config — not edited
after the fact.

## Any market, not just FX

The engine only sees a normalized OHLCV frame (`time, open, high, low, close,
volume`), so AAPL / QQQ daily bars or BTC-USD work exactly like an FX pair.
Export a CSV (e.g. via yfinance) and run the same pipeline.
